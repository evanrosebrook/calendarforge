# Days-between calculator distribution plan

## Goal and positioning

Promote the canonical `/date-calculator/days-between` page as a precise, transparent calendar-date calculator. Its useful differentiators are the exact day total, separate Monday–Friday and weekend totals, calendar-span decomposition, explicit inclusive/exclusive rules, quick ranges, and share/print actions.

Do not describe the weekday result as a business-day result: it deliberately does not remove public holidays. Link people who need holiday-aware answers to the business-days calculator.

## Why this page

- CalendarForge's longer Search Console history contains one click from 22 impressions for the query `days between calculator`, the clearest verified demand signal among the calculator routes.
- The page is already indexed, canonical, server-rendered, and linked to the surrounding calculator cluster.
- Current competitors commonly expose weekday/weekend breakdowns, preset ranges, multiple result representations, and detailed counting explanations. CalendarForge can compete on clarity without adding a broad new route family. Useful comparison pages include [Timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/date/duration.php), [Dates and Days](https://datesanddays.com/), [Omni Calculator](https://www.omnicalculator.com/everyday-life/days-between-dates), and [Days Between Dates Calculator](https://www.daysbetweendatescalculator.com/).

## Two-week distribution test

### 1. Curated resource-page outreach

Build a list of 15 independently maintained pages that already teach date arithmetic, schedule planning, spreadsheet date formulas, classroom elapsed-time exercises, or deadline calculation. Keep only pages where a transparent browser-based result is a genuine addition to the existing guide.

Send no more than five individualized notes per week. Each note should identify the exact section where the calculator helps, mention one relevant capability, and make clear that there is no obligation to link. Do not use bulk outreach or offer payment for links.

Suggested note:

> I found your guide to [specific topic] while checking how people explain date intervals. CalendarForge's free days-between tool now shows total days, weekdays, weekends, and inclusive versus standard counting on one shareable result. If a worked calculator would help readers in your [specific section], it is at https://calendarforge.net/date-calculator/days-between. No worries if it is not a fit.

### 2. One useful community demonstration

Prepare one short worked example for a community where project planning, spreadsheets, teaching, or date arithmetic is already discussed. Lead with the counting ambiguity the example resolves rather than a product announcement. Post only after checking the community's self-promotion rules and obtaining approval for the specific account and destination.

Example structure:

1. Show a date range that crosses a weekend.
2. Compare standard and inclusive totals.
3. Show why weekdays differ from holiday-aware business days.
4. Link the calculator once as the reproducible result.

### 3. Relevant owned placements

Add the canonical calculator URL to an existing public profile, project page, or documentation index only where CalendarForge is already represented. Do not create duplicate directory listings or low-quality profile links. Use one sentence that accurately describes the weekday/weekend split.

## Send readiness

`calendarforge.net` publishes Cloudflare Email Routing MX records and an SPF record, so inbound routing is configured at the domain level. DNS does not reveal which aliases exist, where they forward, or an outbound sender. Before submitting a form, confirm all three of the following:

1. the human sender name;
2. the email address to enter in the form and receive replies; and
3. that a test message to that address is received.

After those checks, present the exact first-wave destinations and final messages for approval immediately before submission. Log each approved submission date and response beneath the queue; never infer approval for the remaining targets from approval of one message.

## Decision gate

Review 14 days after the first approved placement and again at 30 days. Continue only if the test produces at least one of these signals:

- two relevant editorial links or mentions;
- 25 referral sessions with at least a 20% engagement rate; or
- five days-between `calculator_result` events attributable to referral or organic traffic.

If none occurs, stop outreach and use Search Console queries and landing pages to choose a different distribution angle. Do not add generated landing pages to compensate for weak distribution.
