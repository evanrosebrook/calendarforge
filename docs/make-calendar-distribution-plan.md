# Make-calendar distribution plan

## Goal and positioning

Promote the canonical `/make-calendar` page as a free, no-account calendar builder for people who need a clean printable calendar rather than a scheduling service or photo-design product.

The useful differentiators are:

- one, three, six, or twelve consecutive months starting in any month;
- Sunday or Monday starts, week numbers, U.S. or Canadian national holidays, date notes, themes, Letter or A4 paper, and portrait or landscape layouts;
- a shareable URL that preserves the configuration; and
- PDF, SVG, ICS, CSV, and XLSX exports without an account.

Do not describe CalendarForge as a collaborative scheduling app or photo-calendar service. Do not promise support for holidays outside the United States and Canada.

## Why this page

- Search Console recorded 118 impressions for `/make-calendar` during September 27–October 3, 2026, representing 95% of the site's 124 impressions.
- The leading query was `calendar maker` with 76 impressions, followed by `make a calendar` with ten and `calender maker` with nine.
- Average site position was 23.9 and the page recorded no clicks, so authority and discovery are the current constraints; a title-only CTR test is premature.
- The page is indexed, canonical, server-rendered, linked from the global navigation, and already exposes useful print, share, and export actions.

## Distribution rules

- Send no more than five individualized editorial notes per week and no more than one note per publisher.
- Use `hello@calendarforge.net` as the reply address on forms. It is an active Cloudflare Email Routing alias forwarding to the verified destination inbox.
- Use the clean canonical URL in editorial outreach. Directory listings may use either the product route or the domain when the directory requires a homepage.
- Do not buy links, featured placement, priority review, or sponsored coverage as part of this test.
- Do not create third-party accounts solely to increase the submission count.
- Abandon any site that redirects to deceptive downloads or unrelated advertisements.

## Measurement

Review seven days after the first wave and again after 14 days. Record:

- published listings or editorial mentions;
- referral sessions and engaged sessions by source;
- `/make-calendar` landing sessions;
- `calendar_print`, `calendar_share`, and `calendar_export` actions attributable to the builder; and
- Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position for `/make-calendar` and its calendar-maker queries.

Continue only if the first wave produces at least one of these signals within 14 days:

- one approved listing or relevant editorial mention;
- 25 referral sessions with at least a 20% engagement rate;
- five builder print, share, or export actions attributable to referral or organic traffic; or
- a sustained improvement in `/make-calendar` impressions or average position.

If none occurs, stop directory submissions and choose the next distribution angle from live Search Console queries instead of adding generated landing pages.
